Read and follow [agent.md](agent.md) before working in this repository. [README.md](README.md) is the human-facing project overview.

## ICEIB paper workflow

- Treat `myRef/` as read-only. Record new material in [the reference inventory](papers/REFERENCE-INVENTORY.md), including its hash and its role as original, derived, or external evidence.
- Follow [the paper requirements and dependency map](research/academic-plan/EXECUTION.md). Keep the seven numbered changes independently reviewable and wait for explicit approval before drafting manuscript text or running the planned experiments. Finalize the abstract after reviewing the body and the verified evidence.
- Use formal academic English for the manuscript and Traditional Chinese for status and explanations. Trace factual, numerical, and bibliographic claims to an exact source location or version. Track the snapshot audit, proposed methods, hypotheses, planned evaluations, and completed results separately.
- Keep originals and AI analyses in their existing locations. Verify the applicable ICEIB track and format before claiming submission compliance.
