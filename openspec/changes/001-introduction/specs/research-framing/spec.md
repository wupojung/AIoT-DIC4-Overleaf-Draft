# research-framing

## Purpose

Establish reviewable acceptance criteria for the introduction deliverable of an evidence-grounded IEEE-format housing comparison paper.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Research question and scope

The introduction SHALL state background, motivation, problem, objectives, and research questions. It SHALL identify Taichung as a candidate until the study scope is approved.

#### Scenario: Research question and scope review

- **WHEN** The study population is provisional
- **THEN** The introduction limits the scope and frames the fixed versus adaptive workflow as a testable question

### Requirement: Gap and contribution alignment

The introduction SHALL tie the research gap to the compared literature and trace each contribution to a design or evidence deliverable. It SHALL describe the final paper organization.

#### Scenario: Gap and contribution alignment review

- **WHEN** The citation matrix and manuscript outline are reviewed
- **THEN** Each gap or contribution has support and the organization reflects the actual section order
