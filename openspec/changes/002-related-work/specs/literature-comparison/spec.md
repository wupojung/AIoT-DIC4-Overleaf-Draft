# literature-comparison

## Purpose

Establish reviewable acceptance criteria for the related work deliverable of an evidence-grounded IEEE-format housing comparison paper.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Comparable evidence

The literature review SHALL classify housing analytics and tool-using agents, compare methods and settings, and distinguish claims verified in the original publication from unreviewed leads.

#### Scenario: Comparable evidence review

- **WHEN** Studies use different populations, outcomes, or protocols
- **THEN** The comparison identifies those differences and avoids cross-study performance rankings

### Requirement: Bounded gap

The review SHALL explain existing limitations and frame a source-supported research gap linking an identical-evidence workflow comparison to the proposed work.

#### Scenario: Bounded gap review

- **WHEN** An apparent gap is based on an AI summary or absence of evidence
- **THEN** The review seeks a primary source, narrows the claim, or marks the question unresolved
