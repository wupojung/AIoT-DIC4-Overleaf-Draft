# evidence-bounded-conclusions

## Purpose

Establish reviewable acceptance criteria for the conclusion deliverable of an evidence-grounded IEEE-format housing comparison paper.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Findings within scope

The conclusion SHALL summarize the research and distinguish verified findings, proposed contributions, limitations, and future work.

#### Scenario: Findings within scope review

- **WHEN** Only the feasibility audit is verified
- **THEN** The conclusion limits findings to that audit and labels prototype evaluation and valuation as future work

### Requirement: Cross-section consistency

The conclusion SHALL reconcile each claim with the design or experiment and preserve the population and period restrictions.

#### Scenario: Cross-section consistency review

- **WHEN** A conclusion would imply market-wide representativeness or Agent improvement
- **THEN** The claim is scoped to supported evidence or removed
