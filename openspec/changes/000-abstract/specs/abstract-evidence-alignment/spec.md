# abstract-evidence-alignment

## Purpose

Establish reviewable acceptance criteria for the abstract deliverable of an evidence-grounded IEEE-format housing comparison paper.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Abstract coverage

The abstract SHALL cover background, problem, proposed method, evaluation approach, evidence-supported findings, and contribution in formal academic English. Index Terms SHALL follow the verified venue instructions.

#### Scenario: Abstract coverage review

- **WHEN** The approved body sections are available
- **THEN** The abstract presents all six elements and its keywords match the paper scope

### Requirement: Findings consistency

Every quantitative or outcome statement SHALL match the approved evidence and section wording. Pending work SHALL be identified explicitly.

#### Scenario: Findings consistency review

- **WHEN** The paired Agent evaluation has no verified runs
- **THEN** The abstract labels the experiment as planned and reports the snapshot audit only within its observed scope
